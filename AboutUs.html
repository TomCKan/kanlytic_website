About Us

Founded and led by Tom Kanchanatheera, Kanlytic is passionate about turning data into actionable answers that help businesses thrive. With a strong foundation in data analysis and a proven track record across diverse sectors including culture & heritage, finance, and retail, we combine technical expertise with real-world business intelligence and understanding to deliver results that matter.

Building on this foundation, Tom has also completed the Oxford Artificial Intelligence Programme, earning recognition as an OxEthica Certified AI Professional. This ensures our approach to AI and automation is not only cutting-edge but also responsible, transparent, and ethically grounded.

Our services go beyond just numbers. We specialize in process automation, data collection, cleaning, analysis, and visualization to streamline your operations and uncover opportunities for growth. Whether you’re a small business or a growing enterprise, we tailor our solutions to your unique challenges and goals. Data should empower, not overwhelm, and we work closely with you to ensure insights are practical and easily understood.

Partner with us to transform your data into a powerful business advantage, because informed decisions lead to confident growth.
