Supporting Smaller Firms

We believe smaller firms shouldn't have to pay the same price as larger organisations for access to the same data.

While every subscriber receives the full AccountingReach dataset, smaller firms naturally have fewer people, clients and opportunities to make use of it. That's why we offer discounted pricing for smaller practices, making the service more accessible without limiting what's included.

As your firm grows, your subscription can simply move to the next pricing band.

Not Sure Which Pricing Band Applies?

Most firms can choose the right pricing band based on their approximate size. If you're unsure, don't worry, we'll help.

When setting up your subscription, we may use publicly available information, such as Companies House filings, employee numbers or other indicators of organisational size, together with the information you provide to confirm the most appropriate pricing band.

If we think another pricing band would be a better fit, we'll always discuss it with you before your subscription is set up or renewed.

Need the Data for a One-Off Project?

If you only need the data for a specific project or campaign rather than ongoing access, we also offer one-off dataset purchases.

These are priced separately depending on your requirements. Just get in touch and we'll talk you through the options.

Our Pricing Promise

We believe pricing should be simple, transparent and fair.

Every subscriber receives exactly the same AccountingReach dataset, updates and insights. Our standard subscription price is adjusted for smaller firms through discounted pricing, helping make AccountingReach accessible to practices at every stage of growth.

If your firm's size changes over time, we'll simply move your subscription to the appropriate pricing band after discussing it with you first. There are no surprise price increases or hidden charges.
